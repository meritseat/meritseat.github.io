📄 Documents Required for TNEA Counselling (Complete Checklist 2026) 🎯

📅 9 April 2026 ✍️ Meena Saravanan ⏱️ 1 min read

If you’re applying through Tamil Nadu Engineering Admissions "https://www.tneaonline.org/", having the correct documents ready is very important to avoid last-minute stress 😓. This guide will help you prepare a complete checklist so you don’t miss anything during counselling 👇

🧾 Mandatory Documents for TNEA Counselling

Make sure you keep both original and photocopies of the following:

**📌 Academic Documents:**

* ✅ 10th Mark Sheet (SSLC) * ✅ 12th Mark Sheet / HSC * ✅ Transfer Certificate (TC) * ✅ Conduct Certificate

**🆔 Identity & Personal Documents:**

* ✅ Aadhaar Card * ✅ Passport Size Photographs (recent)

**🏠 Address & Nativity Proof:**

* ✅ Nativity Certificate (if applicable) * ✅ Community Certificate (for reservation category)

**📊 TNEA Specific Documents:**

* ✅ TNEA Application Form Printout * ✅ Rank Card * ✅ Counselling Call Letter

**⚠️ Special Category Documents (If Applicable):**

If you belong to special categories, you must submit additional proof:

* 🪖 Ex-Servicemen Quota – Relevant certificate * ♿ Differently Abled – Medical certificate * 🏅 Sports Quota – Participation certificates

📁 Important Tips to Prepare Documents

* ✔️ Keep 2–3 sets of photocopies * ✔️ Arrange documents in a proper file/folder 📂 * ✔️ Ensure all names & details match exactly * ✔️ Carry originals for verification * ✔️ Scan and keep digital copies (backup) 💻

❌ Common Mistakes to Avoid

* 🚫 Forgetting original certificates * 🚫 Mismatch in name/spelling * 🚫 Expired or invalid certificates * 🚫 Missing community proof for reservation

* ✔️ All documents ready * ✔️ Photocopies prepared * ✔️ College list shortlisted * ✔️ Cutoff analysis done
