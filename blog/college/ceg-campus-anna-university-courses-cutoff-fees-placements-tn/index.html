🏫CEG Campus Anna University – Courses, Cutoff, Fees & Placements (TNEA Guide)

🎓 CEG Campus – Anna University: A Top Choice in TNEA Counselling

Choosing the right engineering college is a crucial step for every student participating in Tamil Nadu Engineering Admissions (TNEA). Among the top institutions, the College of Engineering, Guindy (CEG Campus) stands out as one of the most prestigious and competitive colleges under Anna University.

🌟 Why CEG Campus is a Dream College for TNEA Aspirants

CEG Campus, located in Chennai, is one of the oldest engineering colleges in India with a legacy of excellence. It consistently ranks among the top choices in TNEA counselling due to: ​ * High academic reputation * Excellent placement records * Strong alumni network * Affordable government fee structure ​ For students aiming for top branches like CSE or ECE, CEG is often the first preference in TNEA choice filling.

📊 Courses Offered and Cutoff Trends (TNEA)

CEG offers a wide range of undergraduate engineering programs such as: ​ **🏫 Core Engineering Departments** * Computer Science and Engineering (CSE) 💻 * Electronics and Communication Engineering (ECE) 📡 * Electrical and Electronics Engineering (EEE) ⚡ * Mechanical Engineering 🛠️ * Civil Engineering 🏗️ * Industrial Engineering ⚙️ * Manufacturing Engineering 🏭 * Printing Technology 🖨️ ​ **🔬 Specialized Engineering & Technology Departments** * Information Technology (IT) 🌐 * Biomedical Engineering 🧬 * Mining Engineering ⛏️ * Geoinformatics Engineering 🌍 ​ **🧪 Science & Humanities Departments** * Mathematics ➗ * Physics 🔭 * Chemistry ⚗️ * English 📘 * Humanities and Social Sciences 🧠 ​ **🏗️ Architecture & Planning (CEG Campus)** * Architecture (B.Arch) 🏛️ * Planning 🗺️ ​ **Cutoff Insight:** ​ In TNEA counselling, CEG typically has very high cutoff marks, especially for top branches: ​ * CSE: ~199 – 200 * ECE: ~198 – 199 * EEE: ~197 – 198

💡 Key Insight: These trends make it clear that only top-ranking students secure admission here.

💰 Fee Structure at CEG Campus

One of the biggest advantages of studying at CEG Campus is its affordable fee structure, as it is a government institution under Anna University. ​ **Approximate Fees:** Tuition Fee: ₹8,000 – ₹12,000 per year Special Fees: ₹10,000 – ₹20,000 per year Hostel Fees (optional): ₹30,000 – ₹50,000 per year ​ Compared to private engineering colleges, CEG offers high-quality education at a very low cost, making it an excellent ROI choice for students.

💼 Placement Opportunities and Career Growth

CEG Campus has a strong placement record with top companies visiting every year. Students from branches like CSE and IT receive excellent offers. ​ **Top Recruiters Include:** * TCS * Infosys * Wipro * Amazon * Microsoft ​ **Highlights:** * Average package: ₹6–10 LPA * Top packages: ₹20+ LPA (for top performers) * Core companies for Mechanical, Civil, and EEE ​ This makes CEG one of the best placement colleges in TNEA counselling.

🏫 Campus Life and Facilities

The CEG Campus provides a vibrant student experience with: ​ * Large green campus in Chennai * Well-equipped laboratories and libraries * Sports complexes and cultural clubs * Technical events like symposiums and hackathons ​ Students get a balanced academic and extracurricular environment, helping in overall personality development.

🎯 Key Note for TNEA Students

During Tamil Nadu Engineering Admissions (TNEA), the most preferred departments are: ​ * CSE 💻 * IT 🌐 * ECE 📡 ​ These branches usually have very high cutoff scores in CEG.
